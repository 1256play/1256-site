Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 · Türkçe

This policy explains what information the 1256 mobile game ("the Game"), developed by Fuat Suiçer, processes. Contact: info@1256game.com

In short

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or location.
  • Your progress (records, coins, purchased items, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • If you pick a photo from your gallery to dress up the jelly, it is resized and stored only on your device; it is never uploaded and is deleted when you delete the Game.
  • The service providers below process limited information for ads, purchases and game statistics.
  • We do not sell your information.

Information processed and service providers

Ads: Google AdMob

To show ads, Google may process your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent to personalised ads when the Game starts. On iOS, tracking permission is requested through Apple's prompt; if you decline you see non-personalised ads. Details: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases: Apple, Google and RevenueCat

Payments are processed by the App Store or Google Play; we never receive your card details. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID so you can restore purchases. Details: revenuecat.com/privacy

Game statistics: Google Firebase Analytics

To improve the Game, anonymous usage information may be collected, such as which walls are hard, session length and which features are used. It is not used to identify you personally. Details: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Notifications

If you allow it, the Game schedules local reminders on your device (for example "your wheel is ready"). They are created on the device and no information is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the Game's Settings or in your device settings.

How information is used

To show ads, verify and restore purchases, keep the Game working and improve it.

Retention and deletion

Your progress stays on your device and is deleted when you delete the Game. Information held by service providers is kept under their own policies. To request deletion of data associated with you, write to info@1256game.com; we delete everything we can within 30 days.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

Under GDPR and similar laws you can request access to, correction of and deletion of your data. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in your device settings. Requests: info@1256game.com

Changes

If this policy changes, the current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
